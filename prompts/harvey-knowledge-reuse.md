# Institutional knowledge reuse

Search only the approved knowledge base. Identify the most relevant precedent or playbook sections, their dates and limitations. Explain why each applies, flag superseded or conflicting material and prepare a reusable starting structure. Do not expose another matter's confidential content or present a precedent as automatically suitable.
