# Litigation preparation

Using only the approved synthetic matter sources, prepare:

1. a dated chronology with a source citation for every event;
2. an issue and evidence matrix showing supporting, contrary and missing material;
3. a list of disputed facts, assumptions and unresolved questions;
4. potential argument themes and counterarguments, clearly marked for lawyer review; and
5. a citation-verification checklist.

Do not predict the court result, invent evidence or treat the output as final advocacy. A qualified lawyer must verify every material proposition and decide strategic use.
