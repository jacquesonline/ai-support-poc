# CIO demonstration cheat sheet

## Opening

I identified an opportunity to improve how lawyers, partners and staff get support and use AI in day-to-day legal work. I designed a controlled operating model, then implemented it as a working POC. It begins with matter opening, extends across legal-technology support and Harvey, harvests effective prompts and skills, and uses ongoing quality, risk and cost evidence to decide what remains active.

## Narrative

- **What I identified:** repeated chasing, inconsistent diagnosis, unclear ownership, avoidable rework and disconnected AI pilots.
- **What I designed:** frame, ground, propose, constrain, decide and measure.
- **What I implemented:** six support cases, four Harvey patterns, prompts, skills, regression, approval, audit and scorecards.
- **What the working system does:** checks evidence, prepares actions, enforces controls, records decisions and evaluates changes.
- **What evidence it produces:** support outcomes and control results now; for Harvey, evidence that each use case has the sources, output contract, lawyer review, measures and stops required for a controlled pilot.
- **My operating model and release rules:** harvest, version, evaluate, approve, monitor, then continue, change, replace, roll back, retire or stop.

## Matter-opening proof

The matter is approved, but the lawyer cannot find it or record time. The POC reads the request, checks authoritative evidence, distinguishes creation, reconciliation and recovery, stops unsafe actions, routes exceptions to accountable owners and measures ready-to-work time, first-time-right completion, chasing and harmful shortcuts.

## Language to use

- “I built the support POC…”
- “What I designed…”
- “What I implemented…”
- “My operating model…”
- “My release rule…”
- “The system proof is…”

## Closing

The point is not to add AI everywhere. It is to create a repeatable way to identify useful opportunities, implement them with controls, reuse what works and stop spending when the evidence no longer supports the capability.
