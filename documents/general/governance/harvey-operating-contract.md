# Harvey operating contract for the demonstration

ABL publicly announced an agreement with Harvey following a trial and co-development period. This demo extends that public context with synthetic use cases; it does not represent ABL's private Harvey configuration, content, permissions, adoption or results.

## Decision rights

- Harvey may assist with analysis, extraction, drafting and reusable workflows over approved sources.
- Lawyers remain responsible for legal judgement, source verification, advice, filings, advocacy and client communication.
- Matter and information-barrier systems determine access; prompts cannot widen it.
- Practice and knowledge owners approve prompts, workflows, precedents and evaluation sets.
- The Digital Systems Lead oversees support, adoption, change, relevance, incidents, usage and cost evidence.

## Outcome boundaries

- **Court:** measure preparation completeness, source integrity, issue coverage and review corrections; never promise a result.
- **Profit:** measure capacity released, fixed-fee performance, write-downs and realised work; never equate nominal time saved with profit automatically.
- **Cost:** include licence, integration, training, review, support, governance, incidents and opportunity cost.
- **Scale:** require representative reviewed outcomes, zero critical incidents, an accountable owner and a proportionate total-cost case.
