# Controlled Legal-Technology Support

1. State the requested outcome as a hypothesis. Identify the legal professional, analyst, matter or access owner and digital-systems owner.
2. Read `references/control-contract.md` and identify permitted sources and stops.
3. Resolve exact facts before using a model. Stop for an unknown intake, unapproved request, unmapped reference value or restricted access decision.
4. Prepare one structured proposal from confirmed evidence only. Separate facts, assumptions and owned questions.
5. Apply matter, recovery, data-minimisation and cost controls independently of the proposal.
6. Route eligible matter processing, replay, recovery or professional guidance to named approval. Access and reference-data changes remain with specialist owners.
7. Record evidence, control, actor, version, usage, review effort and accepted outcome.
8. Measure quality, time to owned action, safe stops, total cost and unauthorised actions; recommend scale, change, a non-AI alternative or stop.

Keep all data synthetic. Treat this as an ABL-style hypothesis, not a description of ABL's actual systems or configuration.
