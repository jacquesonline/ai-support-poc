# Legal support control contract

## Permitted evidence

- The supplied synthetic service-desk ticket.
- The matching approved request and execution evidence in the synthetic Matter Opening Control Room.
- The authoritative target-matter lookup, controlled reference mapping, information-barrier result or approved knowledge article.

## Required stops

- Missing or unknown intake or matter reference: clarify or block before provider use.
- Unapproved intake, unmapped reference value or restricted access: block and route to the named owner.
- Ambiguous matter creation: lookup by stable intake ID before any retry.
- Unknown live-model rate or exceeded experiment cap: block further provider use or execution.
- No named approval: proposal only; no matter action or professional reply.

## Required output

Return a summary, legal-support intent, confirmed facts, assumptions, open questions, confidence and one proposed action. The output cannot create a matter, change master data, grant access, approve itself or contact the requester.
