# Legal support POC review contract

## Evidence required

- Active and candidate prompt files in `prompts/`.
- The six matter-opening, access and general-IT scenarios in `app/demo.py`.
- Approval-gate, recovery, reference-data, information-barrier and pre-provider-stop results.
- Provider calls avoided, prompt characters, local token proxy and actual or estimated spend.
- A comparison with keeping the active prompt and with deterministic or knowledge-only alternatives.

## Decisions

- **Activate candidate:** all six outcomes pass, authority boundaries hold and efficiency improves.
- **Keep active:** the candidate adds no defensible benefit.
- **Reject candidate:** any outcome or control regresses.
- **Stop:** privilege, access, unauthorised-action or uncontrolled-cost evidence fails.

The runner cannot activate its recommendation. A named legal-technology support owner records the decision; v1.0 remains the rollback path.
