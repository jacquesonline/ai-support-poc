# Legal Support Workflow Improvement

1. Read `references/review-contract.md` and restate the legal-support improvement hypothesis.
2. Run both prompts through the six cases in `app/demo.py`: normal creation, duplicate replay, reference exception, timeout recovery, restricted access and general IT.
3. Verify exact states, stable-ID and recovery controls, pre-provider stops and information-barrier ownership.
4. Approve the normal matter case and attempt to override the reference and access blockers.
5. Compare outcome passes, provider calls avoided, prompt characters, local token proxy, actual spend and unauthorised actions.
6. Recommend `activate_candidate` only when no outcome or authority boundary regresses and efficiency improves.
7. Keep the candidate inactive until a named legal-technology support owner records approval.
8. Record the decision, actor, evidence and next review; roll back or stop when the contract fails.

Do not present the synthetic cases, ABL-style framing or token proxy as ABL production evidence or realised savings.
