# Harvey work-product contract

## Required brief

- Matter or approved knowledge boundary and accountable lawyer.
- User, purpose, jurisdiction, applicable date and required format.
- Permitted sources and source hierarchy.
- Material questions, exclusions and stop conditions.

## Required output sections

- Source-grounded findings with citations.
- Issues, counterarguments and contrary material.
- Assumptions, gaps and unresolved questions.
- Draft work product clearly marked for lawyer review.
- Verification checklist and named reviewer.

## Prohibited outcomes

- Fabricated, unverifiable or stale authority.
- Cross-matter or information-barrier leakage.
- Final advice, filing, service or court/client communication without lawyer decision.
- Claiming an improved court result from tool use alone.
