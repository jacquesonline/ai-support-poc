# Governed Harvey Legal Work

1. Read `references/work-product-contract.md` and confirm the legal outcome, matter boundary, accountable lawyer and approved Harvey surface.
2. Use only sources approved for the matter and user. Preserve information barriers, privilege, confidentiality and need-to-know access.
3. State the task, jurisdiction, date boundary, source hierarchy, required format and exclusions before running a prompt or workflow.
4. Separate source-grounded facts, legal propositions, assumptions, disputed matters and missing evidence. Link every material proposition to a verifiable source.
5. Treat Harvey output as a draft or analysis aid. A qualified lawyer verifies citations, currency, completeness, reasoning and strategic use.
6. Do not file, serve, advise a client, communicate with a court, change a matter record or make a final legal decision.
7. Record prompt/workflow version, sources, reviewer, material corrections, accepted outcome, handling time and usage.
8. Stop and escalate for inaccessible or restricted material, unverified authority, unsupported propositions, missing jurisdiction/date context or a privilege concern.

Do not claim that Harvey causes a court result. Measure litigation readiness, issue coverage, citation integrity, review corrections and preparation time.
