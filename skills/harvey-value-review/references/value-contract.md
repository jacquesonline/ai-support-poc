# Harvey value-review contract

## Quality

- Expected issues found and missed.
- Material propositions with verified sources.
- Contrary authority and counterarguments surfaced.
- Material reviewer corrections and rework.

## Efficiency and economics

- Baseline and assisted preparation time.
- Lawyer review time and exception handling.
- Licence, integration, support, training and governance cost.
- Capacity actually redeployed, fixed-fee margin, write-downs or realised revenue where available.

## Governance

- Correct matter and permission scope.
- Privilege, confidentiality, privacy and information-barrier incidents.
- Prompt/workflow version, owner, approval and rollback.

Court outcomes, client outcomes and profit are influenced by factors outside AI. Report enabling evidence and uncertainty rather than causal claims.
