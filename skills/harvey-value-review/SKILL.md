# Harvey Value and Relevance Review

1. Read `references/value-contract.md` and restate the business need, user group, accountable owner and baseline.
2. Use representative reviewed work, including normal, boundary and failure cases. Synthetic cases prove controls, not production benefit.
3. Measure source coverage, citation verification, issue recall, material corrections, review effort, turnaround, adoption, incidents and total cost.
4. For litigation, measure preparation quality and risk reduction—not win rate as an AI-controlled outcome.
5. For profit, distinguish time released, additional capacity, fixed-fee margin, write-downs and realised revenue. Do not convert nominal hours into profit without evidence.
6. Compare Harvey with a precedent, search, knowledge article, rule, conventional automation or no change.
7. Recommend exactly one decision: `keep`, `change`, `scale`, `replace`, `retire` or `stop`; state evidence gaps and the next review trigger.
8. A review can create a candidate asset but cannot approve itself. Record owner approval, version, rollback and cost boundary.
