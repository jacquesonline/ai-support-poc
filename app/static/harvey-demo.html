Harvey use-case demonstrator

This shows how I would turn a legal-work request into a controlled Harvey workflow. It uses synthetic examples, does not connect to Harvey and does not use ABL matter data.

The ambition

Better legal quality. Stronger economics. Better-prepared court work.

These are aspirations for a controlled pilot to test—not benefits produced or proven by this demonstrator. Court results remain the product of facts, law, advocacy and judicial decision-making, not an AI attribution.
