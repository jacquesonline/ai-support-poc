Governed lifecycle

Proof point 04 · sustainable

Harvest what works. Retire what does not.

I implemented the improvement concept as an operating cycle: observe a useful pattern, capture it as a prompt or skill, test it, approve it, monitor it and reconsider it when evidence or context changes.

My operating model

Every reusable asset has a lifecycle.

The POC gives prompts, skills and review automations an owner, evaluation contract, release decision, review cadence, cost evidence, rollback and retirement path.

The harvesting lifecycle.

Reuse is earned from reviewed evidence—not copied from an attractive output.

01ObserveFind repeated need or avoidable rework
02HarvestCapture prompt, skill or workflow
03EvaluateRun normal, boundary and failure cases
04ApproveNamed owner releases or rejects
05ReviewKeep, change, replace, retire or stop

Each asset has a different job.

Together they make effective practice reusable without hiding responsibility.

Prompts

Define the task, permitted sources, output structure, uncertainty and stop conditions.

Skills

Preserve repeatable professional method, verification steps, authority boundaries and measures.

Automations

Trigger regression after change, weekly adoption and quality review, and monthly relevance and cost decisions.

Activation is a governance event.

The active and candidate support prompts run against the same six-case contract. A recommendation cannot activate itself.

Versioned

The active asset and candidate remain separately identifiable and reversible.

Evaluated

Quality, provider calls, prompt proxy, cost and unauthorised actions appear together.

Owned

A named workflow owner must approve activation; rollback remains available.
