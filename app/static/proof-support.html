Turn tickets into owned outcomes.

I built the support POC to help lawyers, partners and staff across matter opening, restricted access and everyday IT. It gathers the minimum permitted evidence, prepares a consistent next action and preserves the accountable owner’s authority.

What the system does

AI prepares. Rules constrain. People decide.

The same working pipeline handles six different support situations while keeping access, policy, master data and communications outside the model’s authority.

One operating pattern across different requests.

The value is repeatable handling discipline, not a chatbot answering everything.
