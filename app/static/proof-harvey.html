Independent synthetic proof

Proof point 03 · controlled adoption

Turn an AI-assisted legal-work idea into a measurable pilot.

This proof does not claim that a particular product produces better legal work. It shows how a proposed legal use case can be given a defined need, permitted sources, expected work product, lawyer review, measures, ownership and stop conditions before testing.

What I implemented

Four pilot-ready use-case designs.

Litigation preparation, research, diligence and knowledge reuse each connect a prompt, reusable skill, source contract, structured output, review gate and measurement plan. No business benefit is marked as proven.

What we aspire to improve

Legal quality, commercial value and the quality of court preparation.

These are hypotheses to test—not outcomes this demonstrator claims. AI-assisted legal work should scale only if lawyer-reviewed evidence shows a material improvement against the current way of working, after risk and full cost.

Quality aspirationMore complete, source-grounded work with fewer material corrections.
Commercial aspirationMore valuable use of lawyer capacity and improved realised economics.
Court aspirationStronger chronology, issue coverage and authority checks—not a promised court result.

Four use cases prepared for controlled testing.

Prompts specify the work, skills preserve the method, source contracts limit the inputs and lawyers retain the consequential decisions.

Litigation preparation

Chronology, issue matrix, evidence gaps and argument framework linked back to matter sources.

Research and drafting

Jurisdiction-aware source table, contrary authority and a reviewable first-draft structure.

Due diligence

Consistent extraction against an approved taxonomy with red flags exposed for judgement.

Knowledge reuse

Permission-appropriate precedents and playbooks with limitations and currency made visible.

What a real pilot would have to prove.

The pilot—not this design review—must establish whether AI-assisted legal work improves accepted work and is worth its full operating cost.

QualityLawyer acceptance, corrections, omissions and source integrity against a baseline
CapacityActive handling and review time released on representative work
SafetyPrivacy, privilege, permission, reliability and unauthorised-action incidents
ValueAccepted capacity or financial benefit after licence, support, training and review cost
