The Demo's Rational

What I built, why it matters.

Use this as the first-person narrative. Show the proof-point pages for context and move into the working system only when demonstrating evidence.

30-second opening

I identified an opportunity to improve how lawyers, partners and staff get support and use AI in day-to-day legal work. The ambition is better legal quality, stronger commercial performance and better-prepared court work. Those are aspirations, not results I am claiming. I designed and implemented this POC to show how ABL could test them responsibly, measure actual outcomes and stop investing when the evidence does not support continuation.

The central evidence boundary

Aspiration

Improve accepted legal work, realised economics and the quality of court preparation.

What this POC proves

A controlled operating model: sources, prompts, skills, human authority, measures, cost oversight and stop rules.

What a pilot must prove

Material improvement against an agreed ABL baseline using representative work and lawyer-reviewed results.

The six-part narrative

What I identified

Matter and IT requests can involve repeated chasing, inconsistent diagnosis, unclear ownership and avoidable rework. AI initiatives can also become disconnected pilots without a controlled improvement lifecycle.

What I designed

One operating pattern: frame the need, gather permitted evidence, prepare a structured proposal, enforce deterministic controls, retain named human authority and measure the accepted outcome.

What I implemented

A six-case support desk, four Harvey legal-work patterns, reusable prompts and skills, prompt-version regression, approval-gated activation, audit trails, scorecards and review definitions.

What the working system does

It processes synthetic tickets, checks evidence and controls, stops unsafe actions, records approvals, evaluates Harvey work patterns and compares prompt versions before release.

What evidence it produces

The support desk produces case outcomes and control results. The Harvey review proves each use case has sources, an output contract, lawyer review, measures and stops before a controlled pilot; it does not invent outcome evidence.

My operating model and release rules

Harvest, version, evaluate, approve, monitor and reconsider. Continue, change, replace, roll back, retire or stop according to quality, safety, useful adoption, relevance and total cost.

01 · Ready to workMatter-opening proofApproved instruction → usable matter or owned exception
The matter is approved, but the lawyer cannot find it or record time. The POC does not guess or blindly retry. It finds the request, checks authoritative evidence, determines whether this is creation, reconciliation or recovery, stops unsafe actions and routes a genuine exception to the accountable owner.
What I was trying to prove

An approved instruction can become a usable matter without duplicates, blind retries or control bypasses.

What it does

Uses stable identifiers, verifies approval and reference data, looks for an existing matter and distinguishes creation, reconciliation and recovery.

Evidence

Approval record, authoritative lookup, control results, named decision, one matter identifier and a complete audit trail.

Measures

Ready-to-work time, first-time-right completion, support touches, chasing and harmful shortcuts.

02 · Owned outcomeAI-enabled support proofMatter, access and general IT requests
I designed one support pattern for different requests: frame the need, gather the minimum permitted evidence, prepare a structured next step, enforce deterministic controls and retain the accountable owner’s authority.
What I was trying to prove

AI can improve handling consistency without becoming the authority for access, matter actions or professional communications.

What it does

Investigates six normal, boundary and failure cases, grounds the proposal and blocks or routes unsafe conditions.

Evidence

Sources used, control checks, questions, provider calls, approvals, replies and the decision trail for every case.

Measures

Accepted quality, owned resolution, safe stops, unnecessary data avoided, handling effort and provider cost.

04 · Keep it usefulContinuous-improvement proofHarvest, test, approve, monitor and retire
A useful output is not automatically a reusable asset. I harvest the pattern as a versioned prompt, skill or workflow, run it against the agreed evaluation contract and require a named release decision.
What I was trying to prove

Prompts, skills and automations can be managed as governed operational assets rather than disconnected experiments.

What it does

Runs active and candidate prompts through the same six support cases, compares results and prevents a recommendation from activating itself.

Evidence

Version identity, regression results, provider calls, prompt proxy, cost, unauthorised actions and the named decision.

Release rule

Continue, change, replace, roll back, retire or stop when evidence, relevance, risk or cost changes.

05 · AccountableValue and responsible-AI proofScale only when evidence justifies it
Quality, profit and court impact begin as aspirations. Responsible AI and economics sit inside the release gate so an initiative continues only when accepted outcomes justify its risk and full operating cost.
What I was trying to prove

Governance and cost oversight can determine release decisions rather than appearing as reporting after deployment.

What it does

Places quality, speed, safety, useful adoption and full cost together and compares AI with rules, search and conventional automation.

Evidence

Controlled runs, accepted quality, safe exceptions, provider use, spend, unauthorised actions and explicit stop criteria.

Boundary

Synthetic minutes are not profit, preparation measures are not court outcomes and pilot readiness is not realised value.

Release rules

Activate

Only after the agreed evaluation passes and the named owner approves.

Change

When corrections, user friction or changed sources reveal a better pattern.

Replace

When a rule, search, knowledge article or conventional automation is safer or cheaper.

Retire or stop

When relevance disappears, cost exceeds value, or privacy, privilege, safety or reliability fails.

Close

The point is not to add AI everywhere. It is to create a repeatable way to identify useful opportunities, implement them with controls, reuse what works and stop spending when the evidence no longer supports the capability.
