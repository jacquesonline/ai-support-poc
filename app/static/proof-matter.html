Synthetic proof

Proof point 01 · ready to work

The matter is approved. Why can’t I use it?

A partner has approval and the team is ready to begin, but the lawyer cannot find the matter, access its workspace or record time. The system establishes what happened and moves the work forward—without creating a duplicate or bypassing a control.

What I designed

One controlled route from approval to a usable matter.

The POC reads the request, checks authoritative evidence, distinguishes creation from reconciliation or recovery, and routes exceptions to their accountable owner.

What this feels like on a working day.

The ticket rarely identifies the failed component. It says the matter is unavailable and the lawyer needs to work now.

Partner

“This was approved yesterday.”

I should not need to understand an integration or chase several teams to get the matter opened.

Lawyer

“I need to record time.”

I need either a working matter or a reliable update telling me who owns the blockage and what happens next.

Support

“What actually happened?”

I need the approved intake, processing evidence and authoritative lookup before deciding whether to retry, reconcile or escalate.

The outcomes this system is designed to improve.

The POC records these outcomes now. In production, targets would be configured from ABL’s measured service baseline rather than invented percentages.

Ready to workTime from approval to a usable matter—or a clearly owned exception.
Right first timeOpenings needing no correction, reopened ticket or controlled-data repair.
Less chasingSupport touches, hand-offs and follow-ups before the lawyer can continue.
Zero harmNo duplicate matter, blind retry, unauthorised access or reference-data shortcut.

What the demonstration actually proves.

It does not claim to reproduce ABL’s matter platform. It proves the operating behaviours we would want around any matter-opening integration.

Resolve the normal case

Confirm approval and authoritative data, prepare the action and retain named approval before execution.

Do not create a duplicate

When the matter already exists, reconcile the authoritative record instead of replaying creation.

Make failure safe

After a timeout, look up the outcome before retrying; route invalid master data to its owner.
