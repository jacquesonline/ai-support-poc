My proposed ABL operating model

Make AI useful. Keep it useful.

I built this demonstration to show how matter operations, legal-technology support, Harvey-enabled legal work and continuous improvement can operate as one controlled system—pursuing better outcomes for lawyers, partners and staff while keeping authority, ethics and cost visible.

The working proposition

AI prepares. Rules constrain. People decide.

Each proof point has its own page. The shared workbench is separate and contains the live controls and evaluation runs.

The ambition · not a claim of achieved benefit

Better legal quality, stronger commercial performance and better-prepared court work.

These are the outcomes worth pursuing. This POC proves how I would control and measure the journey; only representative ABL pilots, reviewed against an agreed baseline, could prove that the aspirations were realised.

Legal qualityAspire to improve completeness, source integrity and accepted work.
Profit and costAspire to release valuable capacity and improve realised economics after full cost.
Court preparationAspire to strengthen preparation—not predict or attribute a court result to AI.
