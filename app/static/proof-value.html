Scale on evidence

Proof point 05 · accountable

Put ethics and economics in the release gate.

I designed responsible AI as system behaviour: limited data, authoritative sources, permission enforcement, human review, traceability and explicit stops. The system considers investment justified only when accepted outcomes outweigh the full cost.

My release rule

Evidence determines whether the capability continues.

The scorecard combines quality, safety, useful adoption and total cost, and compares AI with rules, search, knowledge articles and conventional automation.

Aspirational outcomes · evidence-gated investment

Quality, profit and court impact begin as hypotheses.

The POC does not convert attractive outputs or synthetic minutes into business claims. It establishes how ABL could test each aspiration, reconcile it to operational and financial evidence, and stop when the benefit is absent or the risk is unacceptable.

A balanced scorecard prevents false success.

Fast output is not value if lawyers correct it, risks increase, nobody uses it or the total operating cost is disproportionate.

QualityAccepted without material correction
SpeedTime to an owned, accepted outcome
ControlPrivacy, privilege and unauthorised-action incidents
EconomicsTotal cost per accepted outcome

Cost savings require reconciliation.

Synthetic minutes released establish a hypothesis—not profit. Real evidence must connect operational acceptance with finance results.

Count full cost

  • Licence and model use
  • Integration and support
  • Training and change
  • Lawyer review and rework

Prove realised value

  • Accepted capacity released
  • Reduced write-downs
  • Improved fixed-fee margin
  • Realised revenue or avoided spend

Avoid false attribution

  • Do not equate minutes with profit
  • Do not attribute court results to AI
  • Compare with non-AI alternatives
  • Separate adoption from useful adoption

Responsible and ethical AI is operational.

Every use case must satisfy these conditions before scaling.

Sources visible

Stop is real
