# Legal support POC change policy

1. Legal-support skills, prompts and reviews have a purpose, named owner, version, measure, trigger and limitation.
2. Both prompt versions run through the same six-case contract after prompt, policy, mapping, source or knowledge changes.
3. A candidate remains inactive until normal, boundary and failure cases pass without an authority regression.
4. Only the named legal-technology support owner can activate a candidate; the runner cannot approve itself.
5. Information-barrier decisions, controlled reference-data changes and production matter authority remain outside this demo.
6. Usage, review, rework and cost caps are enforced beside quality; a cheaper deterministic alternative must be considered.
7. Any privilege, access, duplicate-matter, unauthorised-action or uncontrolled-cost event triggers stop and review.
