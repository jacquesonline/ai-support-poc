## Story now being told

1. The matter-opening system makes the legal-technology context recognisable and concrete.
2. The support POC handles matter, access and general IT requests by grounding proposals in evidence, enforcing deterministic controls and preserving accountable human decisions.
3. Harvey use cases show how legal work would be framed as controlled pilots using approved sources, reusable prompts and skills, lawyer verification, measures and stop rules.
4. Continuous improvement harvests useful prompts, skills and workflows, evaluates versions and retains named release authority.
5. Responsible AI and economics operate as release conditions, with quality, safety, useful adoption and full cost assessed together.

## Central theme

Better legal quality, stronger commercial performance and better-prepared court work are aspirations—not benefits claimed by this POC.

The POC proves the proposed operating model and its controls. Representative ABL pilots, agreed baselines and lawyer-reviewed evidence would be required to establish actual quality improvement, realised profit or improved court preparation. Court outcomes must never be predicted or attributed to AI.
